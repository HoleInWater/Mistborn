# MISTBORN UNITY GAME - DEVELOPMENT HANDOFF

## PROJECT OVERVIEW
We are developing a Mistborn-themed Unity game with lore-accurate Allomancy mechanics based on Brandon Sanderson's Mistborn universe. The game is a third-person action RPG with full Allomancy implementation (all 16 metals), parkour movement, and physics-based combat.

## LORE-ACCURATE MECHANICS

### How Metals Work
- Metals are CATALYSTS, not fuel - they connect to Preservation's power
- Mistborn mentally focus on "wells" of energy in their stomach
- Each metal is a distinct mental switch/muscle
- Mistborn can burn MULTIPLE metals simultaneously
- Flaring = burning at accelerated rate for temporary power boost
- Over-flaring damages the body (Tin Savant risk)

### Metal Categories
- **Physical**: Steel (Push), Iron (Pull), Pewter (Strength), Tin (Senses)
- **Mental**: Zinc (Riot), Brass (Soothe), Copper (Hide), Bronze (Seek)
- **Temporal**: Atium (Enemy futures), Malatium (Future self), Gold (Past self), Electrum (Your futures)
- **Enhancement**: Aluminum (Purge), Duralumin (Burst), Chromium (Leech), Nicrosil (Amplify)
- **Time**: Bendalloy (Speed), Cadmium (Slow)

### Flaring Rules
- CAN flare: Steel, Iron, Pewter, Tin (Physical metals)
- CANNOT flare: Zinc, Brass, Copper, Bronze (Mental metals)
- CANNOT flare: Atium, Malatium, Gold, Electrum (Temporal metals)
- CANNOT flare: Bendalloy, Cadmium (Time metals)
- CANNOT flare: Aluminum, Duralumin, Chromium, Nicrosil (Special)

- **Iron Pull**: Q key (while flaring)

- **F**: Parry/Block
