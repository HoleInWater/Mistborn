# NARRATIVE CHAPTERS 21-38 SUMMARY (THE CONCLUSION)

CHAPTER 21: THE SIEGE OF KREDIK SHAW
Speaker: Kelsier
Lines:
- Kelsier leads the diversionary assault on the palace.
- He fights the Lord Prelate, sacrificing himself to reveal the Lord Ruler's mortality.

CHAPTER 22: THE SURVIVOR'S SACRIFICE
Speaker: Skaa Citizen
Lines:
- Kelsier is struck down by the Lord Ruler in front of the masses.
- His death triggers the legendary "Return of the Survivor" transition.

CHAPTER 23: VIN'S ASCENSION
Speaker: Vin
Lines:
- Driven by grief and power, Vin enters the throne room alone.
- She burns the Eleventh Metal (Malatium) to see the Lord Ruler's true past.

CHAPTER 24: THE FALL OF RASHEK
Speaker: Vin
Lines:
- Vin realizes the Lord Ruler is Rashek, the Terris packman.
- She uses Iron and Steel to strip the Atium bracers from his arms, causing him to age rapidly.

CHAPTER 25-38: THE NEW WORLD
Speaker: Sazed / Elend
Lines:
- The Final Empire collapses.
- Elend Venture is crowned King of Luthadel.
- The crew begins the task of building a new government while the mists thicken.
- Sazed begins his journey to find the remaining Keepers and restore the world's knowledge.
- The story concludes with the realization that Ruin is still at large.
