# ENCYCLOPEDIA SCADRIAL: VOLUME I
## The Final Empire & The Lord Ruler's Reign

Scadrial is a world defined by its metals, its ash, and the singular figure who ruled it for a millennium. This compendium serves as the primary lore foundation for the Mistborn game project.

---

### 1. THE LORD RULER (Rashek)
The Lord Ruler, formerly a Terris packman named Rashek, ascended to divinity at the Well of Ascension one thousand years ago. Instead of releasing the power there to save the world as intended, he took it for himself, remaking the planet's orbit and its biology to suit his preservation of order.

#### 1.1 The Ascension
- **Initial Fix:** Rashek moved the planet closer to the sun to burn away the Deepness (a manifestation of the Shard Ruin).
- **Secondary Fix:** Realizing he had made the world too hot, he created the **Ashmounts**—active volcanoes that spew cool ash into the atmosphere to block sunlight, cooling the planet back to habitable levels.
- **Genetic Alteration:** To help humans survive the ash and sub-par food sources, he altered the digestive systems and resilience of the people, creating the Skaa (drudges) and the Nobility (loyalist leaders).

---

### 2. THE THREE METALLIC ARTS
The magic of Scadrial is divided into three distinct systems, all powered by the Shards **Preservation** and **Ruin**.

#### 2.1 Allomancy (The Art of Preservation)
The focus of our game implementation. Allomancy is "End-Positive" magic. The practitioner (an Allomancer) burns a metal as a catalyst to draw power from the Shard Preservation. The metal is consumed, but the energy comes from an infinite external source.

#### 2.2 Feruchemy (The Art of Harmony)
An "End-Neutral" magic. A Feruchemist (primarily Terris people) uses metals as "Metalminds" to store their own attributes (strength, speed, memories) to be tapped later. Nothing is lost, and nothing is gained from an external source—it is simple storage and retrieval.

#### 2.3 Hemalurgy (The Art of Ruin)
An "End-Negative" magic. Hemalurgy involves driving a metal spike through a practitioner of another metallic art into a recipient. This steals the power, but with significant loss during the transfer. This is the source of the **Steel Inquisitors'** terrifying strength.

---

### 3. GEOGRAPHY OF THE FINAL EMPIRE
The Final Empire is divided into Dominances, each with its own capital and purpose.

#### 3.1 The Central Dominance (Luthadel)
The heart of the empire and the location of our game's primary level.
- **Kredik Shaw:** The "Hill of a Thousand Spires," the Lord Ruler's palace.
- **The Keep Walls:** Luthadel is protected by massive walls, guarded by the Steel Ministry.
- **The Slums:** Dense, overcrowded areas where the Skaa live and the rebellion brews.

#### 3.2 The Outer Dominances
- **The Northern Dominance:** Known for its mining and heavy industrial output.
- **The Southern Dominance:** Agricultural hubs that feed the empire.

---

### 4. THE STEEL MINISTRY
The administrative and religious arm of the Lord Ruler's government.

#### 4.1 Steel Obligators
The bureaucracy. They witness all trades, marriages, and legal documents. They are identified by their distinctive tattoos around the eyes.
#### 4.2 Steel Inquisitors
The enforcers. Religious fanatics with spikes driven through their eyes. They possess the powers of many Allomantic metals and are nearly invincible in combat.
