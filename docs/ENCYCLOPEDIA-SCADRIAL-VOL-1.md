# ENCYCLOPEDIA SCADRIAL: VOLUME I
## The Ashen Dominion & The Ashen King's Reign

Ashara is a world defined by its metals, its ash, and the singular figure who ruled it for a millennium. This compendium serves as the primary lore foundation for the Ashwalker game project.

---

### 1. THE LORD RULER (Varek)
The Ashen King, formerly a Valdris packman named Varek, ascended to divinity at the The Wellspring one thousand years ago. Instead of releasing the power there to save the world as intended, he took it for himself, remaking the planet's orbit and its biology to suit his the warden of order.

#### 1.1 The Ascension
- **Initial Fix:** Varek moved the planet closer to the sun to burn away the The Blight (a manifestation of the Shard The Unmaker).
- **Secondary Fix:** Realizing he had made the world too hot, he created the **Ashmounts**—active volcanoes that spew cool ash into the atmosphere to block sunlight, cooling the planet back to habitable levels.
- **Genetic Alteration:** To help humans survive the ash and sub-par food sources, he altered the digestive systems and resilience of the people, creating the Lowborn (drudges) and the Nobility (loyalist leaders).

---

### 2. THE THREE METALLIC ARTS
The magic of Ashara is divided into three distinct systems, all powered by the Shards **The Warden** and **The Unmaker**.

#### 2.1 Metallurgy (The Art of The Warden)
The focus of our game implementation. Metallurgy is "End-Positive" magic. The practitioner (an Metallurgist) burns a metal as a catalyst to draw power from the Shard The Warden. The metal is consumed, but the energy comes from an infinite external source.

#### 2.2 Storecraft (The Art of Harmony)
An "End-Neutral" magic. A Storecrafter (primarily Valdris people) uses metals as "Metalminds" to store their own attributes (strength, speed, memories) to be tapped later. Nothing is lost, and nothing is gained from an external source—it is simple storage and retrieval.

#### 2.3 Bloodforge (The Art of The Unmaker)
An "End-Negative" magic. Bloodforge involves driving a metal spike through a practitioner of another metallic art into a recipient. This steals the power, but with significant loss during the transfer. This is the source of the **Iron Sentinels'** terrifying strength.

---

### 3. GEOGRAPHY OF THE FINAL EMPIRE
The Ashen Dominion is divided into Dominances, each with its own capital and purpose.

#### 3.1 The Central Dominance (Cinderhold)
The heart of the empire and the location of our game's primary level.
- **Thornspire:** The "Hill of a Thousand Spires," the Ashen King's palace.
- **The Keep Walls:** Cinderhold is protected by massive walls, guarded by the Iron Clergy.
- **The Slums:** Dense, overcrowded areas where the Lowborn live and the rebellion brews.

#### 3.2 The Outer Dominances
- **The Northern Dominance:** Known for its mining and heavy industrial output.
- **The Southern Dominance:** Agricultural hubs that feed the empire.

---

### 4. THE STEEL MINISTRY
The administrative and religious arm of the Ashen King's government.

#### 4.1 Steel Prelates
The bureaucracy. They witness all trades, marriages, and legal documents. They are identified by their distinctive tattoos around the eyes.
#### 4.2 Iron Sentinels
The enforcers. Religious fanatics with spikes driven through their eyes. They possess the powers of many Metallurgic metals and are nearly invincible in combat.
