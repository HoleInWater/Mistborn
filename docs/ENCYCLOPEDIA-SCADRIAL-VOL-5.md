# ENCYCLOPEDIA SCADRIAL: VOLUME V
## The Ashmounts & planetary remaking

The environmental science of the Final Empire.

---

### 1. THE ASHMOUNTS
A chain of massive volcanoes created by the Lord Ruler during his Ascension.
- **The Black Sands:** The areas directly surrounding the mounts, where survival is nearly impossible.
- **The Ash-Cycle:** Every week, Luthadel is blanketed in a fresh layer of grey ash, which the Skaa are forced to clear.

---

### 2. SURVIVAL BIOLOGY
The Lord Ruler altered human physiology to match the harsh conditions.
- **The Skaa:** Optimized for manual labor and minimal food intake.
- **The Nobility:** Designed for beauty, height, and Allomantic resonance.

---

### 3. THE MISTS
The supernatural phenomenon that appears every night.
- **The Mist-Wraiths:** Shapeshifting creatures that haunt the outer dominance.
- **The Mist-Sickness:** A recurring event where the mists "snap" potential Allomancers by putting them through physical trauma.
