# WORLD ARCHIVE: CHARACTERS OF THE CREW (VOLUME III)
## THE SURVIVOR, THE STREET-URCHIN, AND THE KEEPER

Establishing the narrative archetypes and motivations of the rebellion's leaders.

---

### 1. KELSIER (The Survivor of Hathsin)
- **Role:** Leader & Planner.
- **Powers:** Mistborn.
- **Logic:** Kelsier operates on a philosophy of "Hope through defiance." He creates the Legend of the Survivor as a psychological weapon against the Ministry.
- **In-Game Usage:** Provides high-level strategic missions and serves as the primary combat trainer.

### 2. VIN (Ven Dell)
- **Role:** Protagonist & Infiltrator.
- **Powers:** Mistborn.
- **Logic:** Vin provides the perspective of the marginalized. Her growth from urchin to god-slayer is the core emotional arc.
- **In-Game Usage:** The player's primary vessel for experiencing the world.

### 3. SAZED (The Keeper)
- **Role:** Historian & Support.
- **Powers:** Feruchemist.
- **Logic:** Sazed represents the preservation of culture. He is the bridge between the old world and the new.
- **In-Game Usage:** Provides lore-based objectives and healing support via `SazedAI.cs`.
