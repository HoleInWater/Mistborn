# WORLD ARCHIVE: THE NOBILITY (VOLUME II)
## THE GREAT HOUSES & THEIR ALLOMANTIC LEGACIES

Establishing the power structures of the elite noble families.

---

### 1. HOUSE VENTURE
- **Colors:** Green and Gold.
- **Specialty:** Wealth and Political Dominance.
- **Key Members:** Straff Venture (Mistborn), Elend Venture (Philosopher).
- **Allomantic Strength:** High concentrations of Thugs and Coinshots.

### 2. HOUSE HASTING
- **Colors:** Blue and Silver.
- **Specialty:** Espionage and Information Brokerage.
- **Key Members:** Lord Hasting (Tineye).
- **Allomantic Strength:** The empire's most powerful Smousers (Copper-burners).

### 3. HOUSE ELARIEL
- **Colors:** Red and Black.
- **Specialty:** Religious Orthodoxy & Logistics.
- **Allomantic Strength:** Primarily Zinc/Brass manipulation of the masses.
