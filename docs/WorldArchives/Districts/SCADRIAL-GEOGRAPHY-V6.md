# WORLD ARCHIVE: THE GEOGRAPHY OF SCADRIAL (VOLUME VI)
## THE 12 DOMINANCES AND THE ASHMOUNTS

A comprehensive guide to the physical world under the Ashen King's reign.

---

### 1. THE CENTRAL DOMINANCE (Cinderhold Area)
The capital region and the seat of power.
- **Topography:** Flat, ash-covered plains surrounded by distant mountains.
- **Climate:** Controlled by the Ashmounts. Permanent twilight.
- **Resources:** Wealth, Politics, and Ministry Central Command.

### 2. THE NORTHERN DOMINANCE
- **Topography:** Rocky and mountainous.
- **Climate:** Cold, with heavy ash-snow in the winter cycles.
- **Resources:** Metal mining (Iron, Steel, Copper) and coal production.
- **Strategic Note:** The primary source of the Empire's industrial output.

### 3. THE SOUTHERN DOMINANCE
- **Topography:** Vast agricultural basins.
- **Climate:** Relatively warmer, sheltered by the northern peaks.
- **Resources:** Crop production and textile weaving.
- **Strategic Note:** The breadbasket of the Empire.
