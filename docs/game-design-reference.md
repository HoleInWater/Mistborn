# Ashwalker Era One - Game Design Reference

*For Unity development - Era 1 timeline only (1022-1025 FE)*

## Quick Reference: The Sixteen Metals

### Physical (Push/Pull)

| Key | Metal | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| RMB | **Steel** | Push metals away from you |
| LMB | **Iron** | Pull metals toward you |
| Q | **Pewter** | Strength, speed, healing, resistance |
| E | **Tin** | Enhanced senses (sight, hearing, touch) |

### Emotional (Mental)

| Key | Metal | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| Z | **Zinc** | Riot emotions (make angry/scared) |
| X | **Brass** | Soothe emotions (calm) |

### Cognitive (Detection)

| Key | Metal | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| C | **Copper** | Hide Metallurgic pulses |
| V | **Bronze** | Detect Metallurgic pulses |

### Temporal

| Key | Metal | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| G | **Gold** | See your possible pasts |
| 5 | **Electrum** | See your possible futures |

### Noble Metals

| Key | Metal | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| F | **Aluminum** | Purge all metals instantly |
| R | **Duralumin** | Burn all metals at once (mega burst) |
| 6 | **Chromium** | Negate enemy's metal reserves |
| 7 | **Nicrosil** | Amplify ally's metal burning |

### God Metals

| Key | Metal | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| T | **Oraculum** | See enemy's next moves (ghost them) |
| Y | **Revelum** | See person's potential future self |

### Time Metals

| Key | Metal | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| 8 | **Bendalloy** | Create time dilation bubble (speed up) |
| 9 | **Cadmium** | Create time dilation bubble (slow down) |

## Player Character: Ember

### Ember's Abilities (as of 1022-1025 FE)

**Core (can do from start)**
- Steel Push (RMB)
- Iron Pull (LMB)

**Learned During Era 1**
- Pewter Enhancement (Q) - learned early
- Tin Enhancement (E) - learned early
- Zinc Rioting (Z) - learned during First Empire
- Brass Soothing (X) - learned during First Empire
- Copper Cloud (C) - learned from Grimshaw
- Bronze Detection (V) - learned from Grimshaw
- Oraculum (T) - stolen from Aldric
- Electrum (5) - after Oraculum runs out
- Aluminum (F) - known but rarely used
- Duralumin (R) - known but rarely used
- Revelum (Y) - near end of Era 1

### Ember's Combat Style

- Shadow wrestling background (street fighting)
- Trained by Darius in Metallurgic combat
- Aggressive, fast-paced fighting
- Uses momentum and momentum flaring
- Later learns political/social combat

## Enemies

### Human Enemies

**Bloodbrute**
- Bloodforged soldiers
- Variable size (6-12 feet)
- Enhanced strength
- Blue skin when angry
- Limited intelligence
- Use any metal spikes for abilities
- Weakness: Coordination/leadership

**Noble Guards**
- Trained soldiers
- Have Metallurgy
- Equipped with weapons
- Organized tactics
- Can have any metal abilities

**Iron Clergy Prelates**
- Priests/enforcers
- Pewter and Copper typically
- Grey robes
- Religious zealots

**Sentinels**
- Bloodforged constructs
- Near-immortal
- Multiple spikes
- Holes in skulls
- Hunt Metallurgists
- Can regenerate wounds
- Superhuman strength

### Creatures

**Mistwraiths**
- Feed on Metallurgists
- Mist-like creatures
- Lurk in caves
- Can consume metals
- Weak to Metallurgy

**Fleshkin**
- Shape-shifters
- Take bodies
- Bound by contracts
- Can be allies
- Have Blessing spikes
- Intelligent

## Gameplay Systems

### Metal Reserves

Each metal has a reserve that drains when burned.

| Metal | Drain Rate | Special |
|-------|------------|---------|
| Steel | Medium | Push force scales with reserve |
| Iron | Medium | Pull force scales with reserve |
| Pewter | High | Drains faster in combat |
| Tin | High | Drains fastest when senses engaged |
| All others | Low | Passive drain when burning |

### Combat Mechanics

**Pushing/Pulling**
- Light metals fly toward/away from you
- Heavy anchored metals pull/push YOU
- Can push off metal buildings
- Can pull enemies with metal weapons

**Flaring**
- Briefly increase burn rate
- Massive power boost
- Quickly depletes reserves
- Used for finishing moves

**Alloying**
- Combine metals mid-flight
- Creates unexpected effects
- Advanced technique
- Example: Steel+Iron creates curved paths

### World State

**Pre-Well (1022 FE)**
- Ashen King alive
- Ash constantly falling
- Mists every night
- Nobles control cities
- Lowborn enslaved
- Sentinels hunting

**Post-Well (1024-1025 FE)**
- Ashen King dead
- Ash increasing
- Mists gone (protecting crops)
- Food scarce
- Bloodbrute unchecked
- Political chaos
- The Unmaker manipulating

## Locations

### Cinderhold

**City Layout**
- Capital city, ~400,000 people
- Outer districts (lowborn)
- Noble keeps (inner walls)
- The Ember Pits (nearby)
- Diocese of Inquisition (center)
- Assembly Hall (government)
- Ashen King's Palace (destroyed 1022)

**Districts**
- **Theatern** - Noble district
- **Vander** - Commercial, lowborn
- **Goves** - Noble, House Venture
- **Confiscreys** - Government buildings
- **Grimreach** - Lowborn slums, later burned

### The Ember Pits

- Oraculum mines
- Near Cinderhold
- Diocese of Inquisition guards
- Prison labor (lowborn)
- Bloodbrute barracks
- Deep cavern system

### Fleshkin Homelands

- Beneath Dominant Mountains
- Network of caverns
- Sacred pools
- Fleshkin settlement
- Later invaded by bloodbrute

## Factions in Era 1

### Cinderhold Assembly
- Rules after Ashen King's death
- Nobles debating governance
- House Venture has military power
- House Elariel has political power

### Darius's Crew
- Led rebellion
- Took control of Cinderhold
- Later formed new government
- Most members survived

### The Church of the Survivor
- Skeptic religion worshipping Darius
- Illegal but widespread
- Helps lowborns
- Sevren was member

### The Set
- Secret noble organization
- Preserve social order
- Existed since Ashen King's time
- Later revealed to be manipulators

## Victory/Progress Conditions

### Era 1 Game Goals

1. **Tutorial Phase**
   - Learn Steel Push
   - Learn Iron Pull
   - Escape abuse
   - Meet Darius

2. **Rebellion Phase**
   - Join the crew
   - Learn combat
   - Complete heists
   - Survive Sentinel attacks

3. **Confrontation Phase**
   - Attack the palace
   - Face the Ashen King
   - Access the Well
   - Witness sacrifice

4. **Post-Victory Phase** (if continuing)
   - Defend Cinderhold
   - Fight bloodbrute army
   - Discover true enemy
   - Restore the world

## Audio/Visual Notes

### Sounds

**Metallurgy**
- Steel push: Sharp metallic ring
- Iron pull: Deep resonant hum
- Pewter: Power surge, heartbeat
- Tin: Sensation of sharpness, visual crispness

**Combat**
- Sword clashes
- Bloodbrute roars
- Sentinel breathing (mechanical)
- Ash falling (constant)

### Visuals

**Pre-Well**
- Grey/ash sky
- Red/brown buildings (iron-rust)
- Dark nobility colors
- Blue metal lines (Metallurgic sight)

**Mists**
- White fog at night
- Brighter when The Warden weakened
- The Unmaker's red tinge (later)

**Oraculum**
- Silver-white beads
- Glowing when burned
- Ghost images of future
