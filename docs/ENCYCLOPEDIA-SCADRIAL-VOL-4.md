# ENCYCLOPEDIA SCADRIAL: VOLUME IV
## House Politics & The Great Game

The political machinations of the noble families are as complex as the Allomancy they employ.

---

### 1. THE NOBLE HOUSES
Nobility is determined by the "Allomantic Legacy"—the concentration of Allomantic potential in a bloodline.
- **House Venture:** Controlling the majority of the Empire's wealth and mining.
- **House Hasting:** Known for its extensive espionage network.
- **House Elariel:** The primary religious leaders among the nobility.

---

### 2. THE KEEP SYSTEM
Every major city in the Empire is built around a "Keep"—a massive, fortified estate where the families live.
- **Keep Venture:** The largest and most imposing, located at the heart of Luthadel.
- **Keep Elariel:** Known for its stained-glass windows and cathedral-like architecture.

---

### 3. THE GREAT GAME
A century-old dance of intrigue, where houses use **Soothers** and **Rioters** to manipulate each other during balls and diplomatic summits.
- **The Ball Cycle:** The social season in Luthadel, where the next generation of Allomancers is showcased and marriages are brokered.
