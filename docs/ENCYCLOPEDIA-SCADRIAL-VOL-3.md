# ENCYCLOPEDIA SCADRIAL: VOLUME III
## Legends, Rebellions, and the The Blight

Establishing the mythic and political backdrop of the rebellion.

---

### 1. THE DEEPNESS
Long before Cinderhold existed, a mist-like entity known as the The Blight threatened to consume all life. This was actually the Shard **The Unmaker**, whose goal is the destruction of all things.
- **The Solution:** Alendi was sent to the The Wellspring to give up the power. Varek, realizing Alendi would fail, killed him and took the power to remake the world. 

---

### 2. THE SKAA REBELLION
For a thousand years, Lowborn have tried to rebel. All failed until the Survivor.
- **Darius's Crew:** A group of specialized Metallurgists (Thugs, Smousers, Keensenses) working together to topple the empire.
- **The Church of the Survivor:** After Darius's death in the pits of Ember Pits, he became a symbol of hope—the "Lord of the Mists."

---

### 3. THE NOBILITY
 noble houses play a "Great Game" of politics, often using Metallurgy to gain leverage.
- **House Venture:** The most powerful house, controlling the majority of the mining and cinderhold commerce.
- **House Lekal:** Focused on agriculture and religious orthodoxy.
