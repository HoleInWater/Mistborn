  - Base 10x multiplier at cycle 1 vs normal Feruchemical tapping
  - Exponential scaling with cycles: P(n) = P₀ × 10^n × e^(-δn) using AllomancyPhysicsFormulas.CalculateCompoundingWithDiminishingReturns()

  - Cycle decay when compounding stops (3s delay, then 0.5 cycles/sec decay)

- Overrides Feruchemist attributeModifiers with compounded values for downstream gameplay systems
- Events: OnCompoundingStarted, OnCompoundingStopped, OnCycleAdvanced for UI/audio hooks
- Boss API: ForceStartCompounding(), ForceStopCompounding(), PreChargeMetalmind() for AI control
- LordRulerBoss integration:
  - Gold compounding always active for regeneration (source of immortality)
  - Steel compounding in Spiritual+ phase for supernatural speed
  - Pewter compounding in Godlike phase for overwhelming strength
  - Pre-charged metalminds (1000 years of storage)
  - DisableCompounding() method — removing metalminds makes him mortal (lore-accurate weakness)
  - Gold compounding provides additional damage resistance (0.05x instead of 0.1x)
